Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 13, 2026

Voice of Achilles values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected when you submit a form through our website.

Information We Collect

When submitting an inquiry or booking request through our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Instagram Handle
  • Details regarding the tattoo you are interested in

This information is voluntarily provided by you through our contact and inquiry forms.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for communication purposes related to your inquiry, including:

  • Responding to tattoo requests
  • Scheduling consultations or appointments
  • Discussing tattoo concepts and availability
  • Providing updates regarding your submission

Information Sharing

Voice of Achilles does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party companies for marketing purposes.

Your information is kept private and only used internally for communication regarding your inquiry or potential booking.

Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect submitted information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Platforms

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as Instagram or external artist pages. Voice of Achilles is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party services.

Your Rights

You may request that your submitted information be updated or deleted at any time by contacting us directly.

Changes to This Policy

Voice of Achilles reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Voice of Achilles directly through the website or associated social media platforms.
